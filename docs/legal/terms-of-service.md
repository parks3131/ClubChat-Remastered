These are the rules for using ClubChat. They are short and written in plain English, because a
document nobody reads protects nobody.

"We" means the people who run ClubChat. "You" means the person using it. Creating an account means
you agree to what is below.

---

## 1. What ClubChat is

ClubChat is a messaging app for university clubs. A club uses it to run its chat, its races, its
calendar, its weekly meetups and its announcements. Members can message each other directly.

It is a tool for organising a club. It is not a place to store anything you cannot afford to lose,
and it is not a secure channel: as the [Privacy Policy](https://clubchatapp.com/privacy) says
plainly, your messages are not end-to-end encrypted and can be read by us.

---

## 2. Who can use it

**You must be 18 or over to use ClubChat.** You confirm this when you create your account.

If you run a club with members under 18, those members cannot have an account here. This is not a
formality we would waive quietly: it is the age the app is rated against in the app stores, and it
is what keeps a private one-to-one messaging surface out of the rules that govern services for
children.

---

## 3. Your account

- **One account, one person.** Use your real name, because a club roster is useless if nobody can
  tell who is on it.
- **Keep your password to yourself.** Anything done from your account is treated as done by you.
- **Tell us if you lose control of it**, at
  [support@clubchatapp.com](mailto:support@clubchatapp.com), and reset your password from the
  sign-in screen. Resetting signs out every other device.
- **You can delete your account yourself**, at any time, from Profile in the app. If you own a
  club, hand it over or delete it first.

---

## 4. What you may not post

**There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users on ClubChat.**

Do not post, send or share:

- **Harassment or bullying.** Going after somebody, alone or with other people.
- **Threats or violence**, including telling somebody to hurt themselves.
- **Hate speech.** Slurs and attacks aimed at somebody's race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender,
  sexual orientation, disability or national origin.
- **Sexual content**, and anything sexual involving a minor, ever. That last one we report to the
  authorities as well as removing.
- **Impersonation.** Pretending to be another member, a club officer, or ClubChat itself.
- **Somebody else's private information.** Their address, their phone number, their photographs,
  their messages, posted without them agreeing to it.
- **Content you have no right to post.** Somebody else's photos, writing or work.
- **Spam, scams, phishing, or anything sold through a chat that is not the club's business.**
- **Anything illegal**, or anything that encourages somebody else to do something illegal.
- **Attacks on the service itself.** Do not try to break into other people's accounts, scrape the
  app, get around a block or a ban, or overload the servers.

**You are responsible for what you post.** A club chat feels private and is not: everything you
write is read by the people in that space, and can be reported by any of them.

**Some of this is enforced automatically.** Every message is checked against a short list of slurs
before it is posted. A small number of terms are refused outright and the message is never sent. A
few ambiguous ones post normally and are reported to a human, because a word list cannot tell a
joke between friends from an attack and a person can. Ordinary swearing is not filtered, and this
filter is not the main protection. Reporting and blocking are.

---

## 5. Reporting, blocking, and what we do about it

- **Block anybody you share a club with, instantly.** You do not need a reason, you do not need
  approval, and the person is not told. Blocking stops them messaging you and hides the two of you
  from each other's search.
- **Report a message or a person.** A message in a club, race or Eboard chat goes to the admins of
  that space. A direct message, and a report about a person, goes to ClubChat's platform moderators.
- **We act on reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.** We aim to answer a report about
  objectionable content within 24 hours, and to remove content and eject the person who posted it
  where that is the right answer.

**What a moderator can do.** Remove the reported message, and suspend the account that sent it. A
suspended account cannot sign in, and is told so with an address to write to. A suspension can be
lifted if it turns out to have been wrong.

**What a moderator sees, and what is recorded.** When a reported direct message is reviewed, the
moderator sees that message and up to five messages either side of it, and the fact that they
looked is recorded. They cannot browse the rest of the conversation. Moderation is not a licence to
read private conversations.

If you think we have got a decision wrong, email
[support@clubchatapp.com](mailto:support@clubchatapp.com) and say so.

## 6. Your content

**What you post stays yours.** We do not claim ownership of your messages, your photos or anything
else you put into ClubChat.

By posting it, you give us permission to store it and to show it to the people you posted it to,
which is what running a messaging app consists of. That permission is limited to operating
ClubChat. We do not use your content for advertising, we do not license it to anybody else, and we
do not use it to train anything.

**Deleting a message removes its text straight away.** What is left is a marker saying a message
was here, so the conversation around it still makes sense. Deleting your account leaves your
messages in their conversations with your name taken off them, for the same reason. This is
described in full in the [Privacy Policy](https://clubchatapp.com/privacy).

## 7. Clubs, owners and admins

Every club has exactly one Owner and can have Admins. If that is you, you are running a space that
other people rely on, and this is what it involves.

**What an owner or admin can do:**

- Approve or refuse people who ask to join, and hold the club's invite link.
- Remove a member from the club, and ban somebody so they cannot come back.
- Read and act on reports about messages in the club's own chats.
- Create and delete races, events, meetups, polls and news posts.
- Transfer the club to somebody else, or delete it.

**What comes with it:**

- **The club's conduct is the club's responsibility.** If members are being abused in your club's
  chats, dealing with it is your job first and ours second.
- **Handle the invite link carefully.** Anybody holding it can reach the club. Rotate it if it
  escapes.
- **Removing somebody is a real thing to do to a person.** Bans are deliberately harder to impose
  than to lift.
- **You cannot read a direct message between two members.** Being an owner gives you no access to
  anybody's private conversations, and it never will.
- **An owner cannot simply leave.** A club with no owner has no way to recover, so the way out is
  to transfer the club or delete it.

Deleting a club removes its chats, its races, its events and its posts for everybody in it. There is
no undo.

## 8. Direct messages

You can message somebody directly if you share a club with them. Nobody deletes anybody else's
message in a direct message, including us, except through the reporting process in section 5, on
reporting and blocking. If you no longer want to hear from somebody, block them.

## 9. Ending it

**You can leave at any time.** Leave a club from the club screen, or delete your account from
Profile. Deleting is permanent.

**We can suspend or remove an account** that breaks these rules, and we will do it without notice
where somebody is being harmed. Where it is a closer call we will tell you what happened and you can
write to us about it.

**We can also stop running ClubChat.** If we ever do, we will give as much notice as we reasonably
can.

## 10. What we do not promise

ClubChat is provided as it is. We work hard to keep it running and we do not promise that it always
will be.

- We do not promise the service is available at all times, or that a message always arrives.
- We do not promise your data can never be lost. Keep anything you cannot afford to lose somewhere
  else as well.
- We do not promise that everything posted by other members is accurate, decent or legal. We remove
  what gets reported and what we find, and we cannot see everything.
- We do not check members' identities. Somebody in a club chat is who the club believes they are.

To the fullest extent the law allows, ClubChat is provided without warranties of any kind.

## 12. Changes to ClubChat

ClubChat changes. Features get added, and occasionally one is removed because it was not working.
If we remove something you were relying on, we will say so in the app.

**The app can also update itself.** We can send a new version of ClubChat's JavaScript to your
phone without putting a new version in the App Store, and the app starts using it the next time you
open it. That is how a fix reaches you in hours instead of after an app review. The
[Privacy Policy](https://clubchatapp.com/privacy) says what asking for that update discloses.
